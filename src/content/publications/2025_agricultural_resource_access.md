Building resilient agri-food systems in the face of climate change challenges requires addressing the gendered barriers that undermine farmers’ adaptive capacity and limit women’s empowerment. This paper examines how inequalities in access to agricultural resources, decision-making authority and information use shape gender-differentiated resilience among farmers in Nepal and Bhutan.
