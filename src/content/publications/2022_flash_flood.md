On 15th June 2021, a massive flood hit the Melamchi Municipality, and the reoccurrence of the flood on 31st July 2021 damaged infrastructures, and thousands of hectares of agricultural land that stranded Melamchi Municipality and surrounding area for several weeks. The scale of the flood event and debris deposition was enormous.
